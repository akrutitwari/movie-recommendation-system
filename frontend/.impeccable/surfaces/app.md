## Direction contract

THESIS: A recommendation is a credits list. The billing block — the compressed
slab of condensed caps at the foot of every one-sheet, where every contributor
is named, ranked, and sized by contract — is already a system for showing who
contributed what, at what weight. CineMatch computes exactly that. The site is
therefore typeset as film publicity. It refuses the category default (dark
poster carousel) and its predictable opposite (light editorial poster grid).

OWN-WORLD: Drenched oxblood ground (#2B0A10 field, #5E1420 lift), bone stock
(#F2EBDD) as the only text colour, struck gold (#E0A33C) as the single
highlight. One light source flattens everything; no neutral ground anywhere.
Ultra-condensed caps for billing, hairline rules as the only divider, no cards
and no boxes. Rank is expressed as type weight and size, the billing block's
own grammar, never as a chart.

STORY: The visitor understands they are being handed a credited result, not an
opaque score. They believe the number because its arithmetic is set in type
beside it. They select up to five titles and read ten credited recommendations.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed oxblood. Title treatment at display scale upper
left, the search field struck across the measure directly beneath it as the
primary action, a hairline rule, then the real billing slab running full width
carrying the mechanism, the catalog, and the similarity-is-not-a-rating
disclaimer as credited lines. Triptych art sits right, cropped hard.

FORM: The Billing Block, candidate 6 of my ordered grounded list; seed key
1ad4b34d.

RAISES: chromatic totality, from Indoor Weather Sun — one light flattens the
whole surface. Decay as state, from Plankton Wake — a superseded result set
fades rather than vanishing. Preloaded next state, from The Vertical Feed —
no region of the surface waits on a spinner.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
